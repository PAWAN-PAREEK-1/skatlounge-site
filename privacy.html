Skat Lounge – Privacy Policy

Developer: Techbizzy · Contact: support@techbizzy.com · Last updated: 14 November 2026

Summary

Skat Lounge ("the game") does not ask for your name, email or location and has no user accounts. You play against computer players on your device; your lists, statistics and settings are stored only on your phone. The game shows ads and offers optional one-time purchases through Google services, which process some data as described below. The game has no server of its own and does not send your game data to us.

Data stored on your device

Score sheets, statistics, League and daily-deal progress, settings and purchase records are saved locally on your phone. Uninstalling the game deletes them. You can also erase them at any time under Settings → Delete my data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game uses Google AdMob to show a banner on the main menu, occasional full-screen ads between games (never during a game) and optional rewarded videos (for a hint, undo or replay). AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions to show, measure and personalize ads and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown; you can change your choice anytime under Settings → Ad settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Buying Premium or No Ads turns full-screen and banner ads off.

In-app purchases (Google Play)

Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details; we only receive confirmation that a purchase was completed so we can deliver it. There are no virtual coins and nothing that gives an advantage in the game.

Cloud save (Google Play Games, optional)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the game may save your progress (statistics, League, settings and purchases) to your Google Play Games account, so you can get it back after reinstalling. This is stored by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; we do not receive your Google account details. If you are not signed in, your progress stays only on your phone.

Children

The game is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 16.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Germany, Austria and the rest of the EU, the UK GDPR, or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to withdraw consent and to object to processing. We do not hold personal data on our own servers. You can erase everything in the game under Settings → Delete my data, change ad consent under Settings → Ad settings, and turn off ad personalization in your Android settings. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority.

US residents: we do not sell personal information for money. Google AdMob may use your advertising ID for personalized ads, which some US state laws call "sharing". To opt out, use Settings → Ad settings or turn off ad personalization in your Android settings.

Contact: support@techbizzy.com. We answer within 30 days.

Kurz gesagt: Skat Lounge fragt nicht nach Name, E-Mail oder Standort und hat keine Benutzerkonten. Deine Listen, Statistiken und Einstellungen werden nur auf deinem Gerät gespeichert. Du kannst sie jederzeit unter Einstellungen → Meine Daten löschen entfernen.

Werbung (Google AdMob): Banner nur im Hauptmenü, gelegentlich Vollbild-Werbung zwischen den Spielen (nie während eines Spiels) und freiwillige Videos für Tipps. AdMob kann Werbe-ID, IP-Adresse, Geräte- und App-Informationen verarbeiten, um Werbung anzuzeigen, zu messen und zu personalisieren. Im EWR, in Großbritannien und der Schweiz fragen wir über Googles Einwilligungsformular nach deiner Zustimmung; du kannst sie unter Einstellungen → Werbe-Einstellungen ändern.

Käufe: werden vollständig über Google Play abgewickelt. Wir sehen keine Zahlungsdaten. Es gibt keine Münzen und keine Vorteile gegen Geld.

Deine Rechte (DSGVO): Auskunft, Berichtigung, Löschung, Widerruf der Einwilligung, Widerspruch und Beschwerde bei einer Datenschutz-Aufsichtsbehörde. Kontakt: support@techbizzy.com.
